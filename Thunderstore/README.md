# Munster Energy Items

![Munster Energy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JohnnyG1234/ROR2ItemPackMod/main/ItemPack/Assets/mpreviewfinal.png)

Adds new items to Risk of Rain 2.

## Items

| Item | Tier | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| Munster Energy | Common | Increase damage by 5% (+5% per stack). Increase movement speed by 10% (+10% per stack). |
| White Munster | Lunar | While you have shield, increase damage and movement speed by 20% (+20% per stack). Without shield, reduce them by 20% (-20% per stack, compounding). |
